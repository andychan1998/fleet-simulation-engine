# Decisions: any bad manifest entry rejects the whole command, so a tick with a
# bad manifest does not advance and adds no cargo. Cargo can't start at its
# own destination. An absent or null manifest means no new cargo.

> {"command":"init","parameters":{"travel_times":[[0,1],[1,0]],"dock_throughput":[1,1],"vessels":[{"id":"v1","location":0}]}}

# the first entry is fine, the second is not: nothing is added, the clock doesn't move
